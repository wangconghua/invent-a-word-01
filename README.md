One original invented English word with a one-line definition.

**jokelag (n.): the laugh that arrives hours after the joke, when everyone else has moved on.**

Everyone has lived it: a joke sails past you in the moment, and then —
in the shower, on the bus, at 2 a.m. — it finally lands, and you laugh
alone. English has "jet lag" for a body out of sync with its time zone;
it had no word for a laugh out of sync with its joke. *Jokelag* fills
that gap. It is built on a pattern English already owns, so it is
sayable, spellable and memorable, and the definition lands in a single
reading with no second sentence and no example.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
(92 characters including the trailing newline — inside the gate's
1–140 character limit).
